How to use it in your next lesson

  1. Open sim-stud.netlify.app and type your topic, e.g. "titration".
  2. Press Full screen to show it on the projector.
  3. Ask students to predict, then move the sliders to test their ideas.
  4. No internet in the lab? Click Download for offline use and copy it to a USB stick.
