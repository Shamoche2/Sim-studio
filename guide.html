Teacher Guide

Step-by-step directions for every simulation. Use them to plan a lesson, or print them for students.

Using any simulation

  • Move the sliders and menus on the right to change the experiment. The live numbers under the simulation update straight away.
  • Animated simulations have Pause (freeze the action) and Reset (start again).
  • Each page has a Key idea summary and three Try this questions for students.
  • In class: ask students to predict what will happen before you move a slider, then test it.
  • The Learning Support bar at the top of every page helps students with dyslexia, ADHD, dyscalculia or low vision: Read aloud, Say numbers, Simple words, Guided steps (one step at a time, with the control highlighted), Easy reading, Focus and Slow motion.
  • Each topic page shows More ways to learn: a matching virtual lab, the molecule builder, the digital human body or the formula helper, where these fit the topic.
  • No internet? Use the offline download.
